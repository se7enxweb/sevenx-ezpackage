# Credits of the sevenx demo content

## Text

The demo content was first written for the ezwebin site of the platform that preceded Exponential. Its text was
revised for Exponential 6.0.15 by 7x for the Exponential Foundation: every product and maker is now Exponential, the
Exponential Foundation or 7x, and every documentation link goes to https://doc.exponential.earth. It is released under
the GNU General Public License, version 2 or later, like the rest of the package.

## Images

The images that showed the logo or the name of the old platform (conference photos in front of its banner, screenshots
of its interfaces, its customer logo sheet and its event slides) were replaced by original illustrations made for this
package: abstract artwork of gradients, glass panels and line glyphs, and drawn mock-ups of the Exponential
administration interface. They were drawn as SVG by a script and rendered to JPEG, PNG or GIF.

The illustrations are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) by their maker, 7x. No attribution is required.
